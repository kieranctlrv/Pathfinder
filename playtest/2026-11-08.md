## Grievances

### 1. Swapped-out part: no "store in hold" option
- Where: Kael Trace offer screen (Sector 1, Passage 3), after taking Heavy Reactor Mk I and fitting it from this screen.
- What happened: the old Standard Reactor Mk I then showed only "Fit to reactor (replaces Heavy Reactor Mk I)" and "Sell: +3 salvage".
- Wants: a "Store in hold" option; fine for it to take a hold slot.
- Note for the fix: the header already reads "the hold has 1 of 6 slots used", so the old part is probably already sitting in the hold and leaving keeps it. The screen doesn't say so, and Fit/Sell reads like a forced choice. Fix is likely labelling ("Kept in the hold", a Keep/Leave-in-hold button) plus checking it really persists after LEAVE.
- Screenshot: playtest/shots/2026-11-08-01-swap-part.png

### 2. No engine sound in transit (Run mode)
- Where: Run mode, during a fold (in transit between passages).
- What happened: no engine/drive sound plays while travelling.
- Note for the fix (2): check whether the Campaign's fold ambience is skipped by the R2.live guards that switch off the real-time sim in Run mode.


### 3. Next Actions fold engages instantly; Set Course still showing
- Where: Run mode Home, Sector 1 Passage 4 (heading to Hostile Contact).
- What happened: clicking a destination in Next Actions ("FOLD: HOSTILE CONTACT") engaged the fold immediately. While under way, the Contacts panel still shows SET COURSE / FOLD buttons, and Next Actions still lists the other fold options.
- Wants: it to make sense. Either Next Actions sets a course you then engage, or once under way the planning buttons go away (or change to "under way" / drop to sublight).
- Screenshot: playtest/shots/2026-11-08-03-next-actions-fold.png
- Related evidence for #1: this screen shows HOLD 0 OF 6, a passage after the Kael Trace screen said 1 of 6. The swapped-out Standard Reactor Mk I looks to have been lost on LEAVE, so #1 is a real bug, not only labelling.
