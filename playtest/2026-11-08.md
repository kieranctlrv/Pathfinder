# Playtest batch: 2026-11-08

Build tested: Build 5 (commit 272a904), played at https://cn4417.netlify.app/
Kept separate from the audit findings and the planned balance patch.

## Grievances

### 1. Swapped-out part: no "store in hold" option
- Where: Kael Trace offer screen (Sector 1, Passage 3), after taking Heavy Reactor Mk I and fitting it from this screen.
- What happened: the old Standard Reactor Mk I then showed only "Fit to reactor (replaces Heavy Reactor Mk I)" and "Sell: +3 salvage".
- Wants: a "Store in hold" option; fine for it to take a hold slot.
- Note for the fix: the header already reads "the hold has 1 of 6 slots used", so the old part is probably already sitting in the hold and leaving keeps it. The screen doesn't say so, and Fit/Sell reads like a forced choice. Fix is likely labelling ("Kept in the hold", a Keep/Leave-in-hold button) plus checking it really persists after LEAVE.
- Screenshot: playtest/shots/2026-11-08-01-swap-part.png

### 2. No engine sound in transit (Run mode)
- Where: Run mode, during a fold (in transit between passages).
- What happened: no engine/drive sound plays while travelling.
- Note for the fix (2): check whether the Campaign's fold ambience is skipped by the R2.live guards that switch off the real-time sim in Run mode.


### 3. Next Actions fold engages instantly; Set Course still showing
- Where: Run mode Home, Sector 1 Passage 4 (heading to Hostile Contact).
- What happened: clicking a destination in Next Actions ("FOLD: HOSTILE CONTACT") engaged the fold immediately. While under way, the Contacts panel still shows SET COURSE / FOLD buttons, and Next Actions still lists the other fold options.
- Wants: it to make sense. Either Next Actions sets a course you then engage, or once under way the planning buttons go away (or change to "under way" / drop to sublight).
- Screenshot: playtest/shots/2026-11-08-03-next-actions-fold.png
- Related evidence for #1: this screen shows HOLD 0 OF 6, a passage after the Kael Trace screen said 1 of 6. The swapped-out Standard Reactor Mk I looks to have been lost on LEAVE, so #1 is a real bug, not only labelling.

### 4. Enemy looks different in combat vs the Home viewer
- Where: Run mode, Sector 1 Passage 4, Hunt I fight against a Cordon Picket.
- What happened: Battle Stations shows the Cordon Picket as a grey chevron/flyer sprite. The Home viewer at the same moment shows a long brown hull with an orange engine glow, labelled only "Sublight at Hostile Contact" and "Battle stations". Two different ships for one enemy.
- Wants: one consistent look per enemy, and a clear way for the player to know what the viewer is showing (enemy vs friendly). Kieran sees this as part of a UI rework.
- Note for the fix: the viewer pulls from the art catalogue's model library by contact/node type; the combat stage uses its own pixel sprites. They need to share one asset per enemy (ties to the art catalogue rule). The viewer also needs a label/tag on the subject (name, faction, HOSTILE).
- Also visible: the 3.4 fold changed to "6.1 in combat" in the Fold Field panel, and Intel still shows "Name: Wreck" during the fight.
- Screenshots: playtest/shots/2026-11-08-04a-combat-enemy.png, playtest/shots/2026-11-08-04b-home-viewer.png

### 5. No animation for the last move; turns aren't clear visually
- Where: Run Battle Stations, Hunt I vs Cordon Picket, turn 3.
- What happened: the round resolved (enemy opened the range, then "Lance array hits. Screen -0, hull -47", Picket down to 13/60) with no animation on the stage. The only record is three lines of feed text.
- Wants: each move animated, and a clear visual definition of turns (whose move is happening, when a round starts and ends).
- Note for the fix: the R2e tactical stage has beam/hit effects; check why they didn't fire on this round (range change first, then the shot). Ideas to raise with Kieran: a round banner ("Turn 3"), a step-by-step playback (enemy move, then yours) with a short pause between, highlight on the acting ship, damage numbers on the target.
- Screenshot: playtest/shots/2026-11-08-05-combat-turns.png

### 6. Second fight starts immediately after winning the first
- Where: Run Sector 1 Passage 4. Folded toward Hostile Contact, the threat crossed 25% and Hunt I (Cordon Picket) triggered mid-fold. After winning it, the Wrecker Raider fight (the original destination) started straight away with no break.
- What happened: no victory screen, no breather, no explanation that this was the contact you were heading to. The win, "+5 salvage", "hunt beaten, threat 34% to 0%" and "Good drop: +9 salvage" only appear as lines in the ship log.
- Wants: the gameplay loop thought through and refined so the flow between encounters makes sense.
- Note for the fix: run-map arriveHunt stores the original card in R.cur.then and continues into it after the hunt. Needs a between-fights beat at minimum: a result screen (win, drop, threat pushed back), then an arrival beat for the real destination ("You reach the hostile contact: Wrecker Raider") before Battle Stations. Bigger question for Kieran: should a hunt interrupt the fold at all, or happen on arrival/instead of the destination?
- Screenshot: playtest/shots/2026-11-08-06-back-to-back-fight.png
